\chapter{Complete result tables}
\label{app:results}
\chaptermark{Complete result tables} %running head

This appendix gives in full the tables that \cref{ch:results} summarises. Unless a caption says otherwise, a value is
the mean over seeds 0 to 9 of the test part, or the median of the paired differences over the same seeds, and a
difference is B minus A. Arms, datasets and experiments are those of \cref{ch:benchmarking-methodology}.
The last section gives the macro-F1 of every seed of the final runs, their accuracy and MCC, and
their precision, recall and F1 per class, and the C5 measures per axiom. The confusion matrices of every run are in its result file.
