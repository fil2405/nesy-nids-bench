\chaptermark{Hyperparameter grids} %running head

\Cref{tab:tuning-spaces} gives the space of each arm (\cref{sec:matched-tuning}). Each arm
is searched with 20 trials of 3 seeds. Ranges are uniform, log-uniform (log) or a list.
Fixed in every trial are, for the neural arms, the network of \cref{sec:impl-mlp} or that of the
1D-CNN, batches of 256 and at most 100 epochs; for XGBoost at most 300 rounds; and for every arm
the selection rule of \cref{sec:matched-tuning}. On CIC-IDS2017 the trials train on a quarter of
the Normal rows.

\Cref{tab:tuning-selected} gives the values chosen by the search and used by the final runs. The
same draw of the Latin hypercube can win on more than one dataset, since the search seed and the
space are the same, and the learning rate $3.4\cdot10^{-4}$ of MLP-CE with weights and of the
1D-CNN on the CIC datasets is one such draw. The two trees on NSL-KDD were searched on the laptop
(\cref{sec:impl-environment}).
